A Different Standard of Care

Why a yearly physical
isn't catching this.

Standard care looks at outcomes after they happen. We look at trajectory.

Approach 01

Standard Annual Physical

Sees the symptoms — once they exist.

  • BMI and blood pressure
  • Standard cholesterol panel
  • Fasting glucose (catches diabetes after it has developed)
  • Sleep, hormones, body composition rarely tested
  • 10–15 min visit, no follow-through
Approach 02

Protect Them Project

Sees the trajectory — and changes it.

  • Visceral fat & lean muscle (DEXA)
  • Cardio fitness via VO₂ max — predicts injury & longevity
  • Real-time glucose response (CGM) — catches insulin resistance years early
  • Hormones, thyroid, sleep apnea screening
  • Advanced imaging when indicated (Full Body MRI, Coronary CT)

Case Study · 01

50-year-old male constable.
Past his prime — or so he thought.

What he presented with

  • Couldn't lose weight despite trying for years
  • Brain fog, low energy, muscle aches
  • Hot flashes, declining strength
  • Felt "past his prime"
  • On a trajectory toward diabetes
What we found that no one had connected

High visceral fat · Insulin nearly 3× normal · Pre-diabetic A1c (5.8%) · Low testosterone · Undiagnosed sleep apnea

4 months later

  • Down 10 lb (on track for 40 lb goal)
  • A1c 5.8 → 5.4 (out of pre-diabetic range)
  • Fasting insulin substantially improved
  • Testosterone restored to normal range
  • Sleep apnea diagnosed & treated; brain fog gone
  • Energy, strength, mood all returning

Foundation Zone 2 + gym training · dietitian-led nutrition · repeat DEXA shows fat down, lean mass preserved.

Trajectory changed: from "early disability retirement" to "finishing his career strong."

A Partnership

What a partnership

This is a first conversation. Here are three options — we're flexible.

01
Option One

Pilot cohort

5–10 interested officers complete a 6-month program. We track outcomes (DEXA, labs, fitness, sick days) and share aggregated results.

Referral pathway

A formal pathway for officers to access the program voluntarily, paid via personal HSA or extended benefits. No mandate, no pressure.

An integrated program offered to all officers, with funding co-developed with the department. Outcomes reported annually.
